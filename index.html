AI Tech Channel Uploader

AI Tech Channel Uploader is a personal, non-commercial tool used by the owner of this site to publish videos to their own YouTube channel about technology and artificial intelligence.

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions and to set their publishing schedule. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
