Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how AI Tech Channel Uploader ("the app") handles data. The app is a personal tool operated by the owner of this site to manage their own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel and to read and update the status of those videos. The app requests only the permissions needed for this: youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, does not collect analytics, and does not store, sell or share any user data with third parties.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via GitHub.
